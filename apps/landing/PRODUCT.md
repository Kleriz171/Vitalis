# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone in Albania, Tirana first: people who saw Vitalis on a poster, in the news or from a friend, mostly
on a phone; people with medical or first-aid training who might become responders; and organisations
(businesses, schools, municipalities, the ambulance service, funders) weighing a partnership. Visitors
read in Albanian or English.

## Product Purpose

The public face of Vitalis, an emergency-response network: when someone collapses, the nearest
certified responder is called, a second one brings the closest defibrillator, and the caller is
coached through CPR until help arrives. The site exists to make Vitalis trusted and wanted.
Success: visitors download the app, trained people sign up as responders, organisations get in touch.
It also hosts the certificate check (/verify), the live tracking link (/track) and the privacy policy.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, close. A steady hand in the worst minute of someone's day: clear before clever,
confident without drama, proud of Tirana and of neighbours helping neighbours. Urgency is reserved
for SOS itself. The visitor should leave thinking "this is serious, and it is for me".

## Anti-references

- Generic health-app sites: stock photos of smiling doctors, floating phone mockups on gradients
  (GoodSAM, PulsePoint and most "emergency app" pages).
- Anything that "looks AI generated": dark neon dashboards, glassmorphism, gradient text, icon-card
  grids, eyebrow labels over every section.
- Fear marketing: sirens, gore, red everywhere, countdown clocks.

## Design Principles

1. Show the mechanism. The idea (nearest responder + defibrillator runner) should be seen, not explained.
2. Real over decorative. Real Tirana, real app screens, real numbers only when we have them.
3. One idea per screen. Pace the scroll; each viewport earns its place.
4. Calm hand, red only for SOS. Colour carries trust; urgency is a single, precise accent.
5. Equal in Albanian and English. Neither language is a translation afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Readable on cheap Android phones and slow connections: heavy media loads progressively
and never blocks reading. Full keyboard paths, visible focus, reduced-motion alternatives for every
animation, no information carried by colour alone. Albanian copy is a draft until a native speaker reviews it.
